What the weekly fields mean
W Week
One Overlord campaign week, keyed by its start. Source "history" = a finished week from Overlord's weekly archive (that week's top 100 players only); "live" = the running week, summed by Overlord Activity from Overlord's ranking. Top-100 HKs (total) is the top 100's kills so live and archived weeks compare. Players = 100 for archived weeks; for the live week, every identity in the ranking. Captures = sum of the top 100's capture counts (not known for the live week). Zones captured = Overlord's total zones captured. Alliance / Horde = HKs whose player faction is known (0 = unknown); in the live week these are summed over the whole ranking, so they can be larger than the top-100 total. Best player HKs = the week's top player's kills (the name is not published).
F Faction sample
Faction HK totals of the running week at a moment in time, taken every 10 minutes (this and last week only). live = Overlord Activity's own sum of the whole ranking, de-duplicated by identity; cache = Overlord's saved login preview, used until the first live sum of a week. Unknown = HKs of players whose faction Overlord doesn't know; players = identities counted.
G Guild
Top 10 guilds of the running week by HKs, with their faction.
M Your place
Your own rank, HKs and killing blows in Overlord's ranking for that week, out of all players counted. Only present when your character was found in the ranking.
L Front last seen
The last time each front was seen active, from Overlord's saved front activity. It can be a time when you were offline.
V Last victory
Overlord's snapshot of the last front victory: the front, allied kills, enemy kills, total kills and the winning faction.
Empty values
An empty number means "not known", never zero. Shown here as "–".

Data comes from the Overlord Activity addon (own 15-minute recordings) and Overlord's weekly network data. HK figures are estimates.

Privacy: the weekly best player's name is never published, and character names are shown as "Character 1", "Character 2"… Guild names come from Overlord's public weekly ranking.
